# Case Studies

These are current proof surfaces for technical review.

Each case study connects a problem, a mechanism, visible evidence, maturity and
reusable value. Public order is selected for comprehension and does not redefine
semantic containment.

## 1. MAIOS / project-centred AI operating environment

Problem: AI-assisted projects lose context, memory, decisions, competence and
direction as models, tools, sessions and environments change.

Mechanism: MAIOS composes a Project Kernel, project context, models, tools,
memory, competences, sources and reviewable state into a persistent operating
environment.

Evidence:

- MAIOS: https://maios.it
- Portfolio card: ../../projects/maios/README.md

Current demonstrated/evolving evidence includes a public setup surface, a
self-configuring project package and an owner-native Codex setup/reentry path.
Broader host validation remains open.

Reusable value: project-centred AI setup, continuity, competence composition,
reviewable evolution and a path from real context to an operational AI system.

## 2. RepoKernel / Project Kernel

Problem: a project needs a stable, reconstructable operating seed without
forcing every AI/coder session to rediscover its context and rules.

Mechanism: RepoKernel acts as a generative metakernel/source; the generated
Project Kernel becomes the situated project operating seed and owner of its
project state.

Evidence:

- RepoKernel: https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/RepoKernel
- MAIOS public component/setup surfaces: https://maios.it

Reusable value: project continuity, source mapping, reentry and generated
project-specific operating structure.

## 3. AI Lab / self-maintaining research cycle

Problem: AI-assisted research often produces fluent outputs without a durable
way to test, remember, block or promote them.

Mechanism: the Lab reconstructs a live field, turns tensions into experiments
or tools, falsifies outputs, applies gates, stores useful residue and updates
the next cycle.

Evidence:

- AI Lab: https://d-nd.com/ai-lab
- Lab dashboard: https://lab.d-nd.com/dashboard/

Reusable value: an operating cycle for R&D workflows where outputs must remain
inspectable and non-promoted until they hold.

## 4. Domain Labs / lab.d-nd.com

Problem: a Lab method must transfer beyond its original domain.

Mechanism: domain + intent + candidate material becomes a Lab surface,
blueprint, cycle contract, dashboard and reviewable operating path.

Evidence:

- Lab surface: https://lab.d-nd.com/
- Live dashboard: https://lab.d-nd.com/dashboard/
- D-ND Lab project/repository: https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/D-ND_LAB

## 5. d-nd-seed

Problem: AI coding sessions lose state, repeat corrected mistakes and restart

Mechanism: Seed packages boot routing, compact recovery, hooks, memory, safety
checks, skills, evals and Lab transfer patterns.

Evidence:

- Seed surface: https://seed.d-nd.com/
- Repository: https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/d-nd-seed

Evidence:

- Public surface: https://seed.d-nd.com/ux-ai/

## 7. D-ND Business Skill

Problem: useful AI work can exist while business context, surfaces, evidence,
opportunities and next actions remain scattered.

Mechanism: the public-clean business competence maps context, surfaces,
opportunities, evidence and boundaries before selecting a bounded next action.

Evidence:

- D-ND Business Skill: https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/dnd-business-skill
- Project-continuity companion: https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/RepoKernel

Reusable value: business continuity around AI projects without exposing private
Business Manager state or implying autonomous outreach/commitment.

Historical note: `AI Business Operating Review` is retained as a superseded
public product identity / lineage, not the current Business Skill name.

## 8. THIA / total-system context

Problem: a growing distributed AI ecosystem cannot be understood correctly if
its nodes, interfaces, runtimes, memory, services and research surfaces are
mistaken for unrelated projects or for the whole system individually.

Mechanism: **THIA is the total-system relation / whole ecosystem context**.
Concrete implementations can instantiate distributed nodes, interfaces,
runtimes, services, agents, memory, routing and coordination functions.

Evidence:

- Main site: https://d-nd.com
- THIA public surface: https://d-nd.com/thia
- AI Lab: https://d-nd.com/ai-lab

Reusable value: whole-system coherence across changing incarnations and clear
separation between semantic system identity and contingent runtime structure.

Architecture boundary:

```text
THIA = total system
D-ND = logical/cognitive kernel and deeper research source
THIAMAN = manager / architect function
Multi_Agent_OS = distributed presentation / execution surface
MAIOS = current project-centred operational/public convergence
```

## 9. Physics/Math Lab

Problem: a generic Lab engine needs a high-friction domain where false patterns,
mathematical attractors and premature discoveries are real risks.

Mechanism: the Physics/Math Lab uses assertions, experiments, falsifier reports,
gate states, non-promotion and redesign cycles.

Evidence:

- Lab dashboard: https://lab.d-nd.com/dashboard/
- Repository: https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/dnd-lab-physics

Reusable value: stress test for falsification, non-promotion and controlled
regeneration.

Boundary: do not present this as a physics-unification proof.
